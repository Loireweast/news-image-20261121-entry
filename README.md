One image summing up the world news of **Saturday 21 November 2026**, drawn as a
newspaper front page. The day's lead is a Russian double-tap ballistic strike on
Kryvyi Rih, President Zelenskyy's hometown, killing a 7-year-old boy and a
20-year-old paramedic and wounding 60 — with Mexico capturing FBI most-wanted
cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro", COP31 grinding into overtime in Antalya, and a
dengue breakthrough in Brazil rounding out a crowded Saturday.

| # | Story | Source |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Russian ballistic missiles hit Kryvyi Rih on Friday night; more rockets struck as rescuers arrived — 2 killed (a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old paramedic), 60 wounded, six of them children | Associated Press · AAP |
| 2 | Mexican forces capture Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro", one of the FBI's 10 most wanted, in Guasave, Sinaloa overnight, with US intelligence help; gunfire and burning roadblocks follow, extraction at 4 a.m. Saturday | Associated Press · Reuters · NPR |
| 3 | COP31 climate talks in Antalya run past their Friday deadline into overtime with no deal; fossil fuels still missing from the draft text | Reuters · Associated Press |
| 4 | Wolbachia-carrying mosquitoes cut dengue and chikungunya infections by about 69% in a four-year trial of 5,700 schoolchildren in Belo Horizonte, Brazil | Associated Press |
| 5 | Paris court sentences three police officers over the racist 2020 beating of music producer Michel Zecler; 1–2 years in prison and a lifetime ban from the police | Reuters · Associated Press · Al Jazeera |
| 6 | Federal jury convicts Jonathan Rinderknecht of starting the fire that became the 2025 Palisades Fire, which killed 12 people and burned ~7,000 homes | NPR · Associated Press |
| 7 | At least 11 dead and 180+ in hospital after drinking suspected spurious liquor in Manipur's Chandel district; methanol poisoning suspected | Reuters · The Hindu |

The ticker also notes 100 civil-society groups warning of the "most
disappointing COP" and Florida facing its biggest US dengue outbreak since the
1940s.

Every story was checked live against multiple sources and dated to 21 November
2026: events from earlier in the week — the 19 November ICJ genocide ruling,
the Shenzhen summit, Cyclone Akvan's landfall, the Kyiv overnight barrage and
the EU–UK summit, all covered in the 18–20 November editions — were deliberately
left out so the page shows only Saturday's news. Stories that happened late on
Friday night (the Kryvyi Rih strike, the Sinaloa capture) are included because
they were first reported on Saturday 21 November.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the repository's `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The image itself was drawn in `tools/scene.html`
(pure HTML/CSS/SVG, system fonts, no network) and rendered to PNG at 1024×1024.
